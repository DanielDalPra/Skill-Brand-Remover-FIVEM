# Raptor grille reconstruction lessons

The request was to remove Ford branding while preserving the vehicle. The rear was approved first and remained unchanged during later grille revisions.

A YTD-only edit left FORD visible because the letters were raised geometry in both main YFTs. Removing isolated letter faces still left letter-shaped outlines in the supporting structure. Covering them with a smooth strip blocked the grille and was rejected.

The approved result rebuilt the open center from the original adjacent cell pattern, preserving its frame, depth, and native styling. An arbitrary rectangular grid did not match the request. Transforming shared vertices without copying them also distorted the donor geometry: clone the vertex records before changing coordinates.

Geometry indices, bones, cuts, and offsets are specific to that vehicle. Do not reuse them on another model. Preserve the exact approved revision and do not reuse its upload destination for a later vehicle.
